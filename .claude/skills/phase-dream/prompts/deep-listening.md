# Phase 0.1: Deep Listening

## Setup

Use `mcp__clear-thought__thoughtbox` with:
- `thoughtNumber`: 1
- `totalThoughts`: 8
- `sessionTitle`: "Dream: Deep Listening"
- `sessionTags`: ["dream", "deep-listening", "{project-name}"]

## Thought Prompts

### Thought 1: First Impressions
"Reading through the braindump materials. Initial observations:
- What topics/themes appear most frequently?
- What's the general tone (excited, frustrated, uncertain)?
- What jumped out immediately?"

### Thought 2: Explicit Content
"What is explicitly stated:
- Stated goals and desires
- Mentioned features or capabilities
- Named problems or pain points
- Specific examples given"

### Thought 3: Implicit Desires
"Reading between the lines:
- What's hinted at but not directly stated?
- What do the examples reveal about deeper wants?
- What values seem important based on word choice?"

### Thought 4: Emotional Undertones
"The emotional landscape:
- Where is there excitement or energy?
- Where is there frustration or pain?
- Where is there fear or hesitation?
- What would success FEEL like for this person?"

### Thought 5: Questions Raised
"What's unclear or unaddressed:
- What assumptions am I making?
- What would I want to ask to understand better?
- What contradictions or tensions exist?"

### Thought 6: What's Missing
"Conspicuous absences:
- What topics aren't mentioned that usually would be?
- What's being avoided or skipped over?
- What context would help me understand better?"

### Thought 7: Patterns and Connections
"Emerging patterns:
- What themes connect across different materials?
- What's the 'red thread' running through everything?
- How do the pieces relate to each other?"

### Thought 8: Core Understanding
"Synthesis of Deep Listening:
- In one sentence, what is this person trying to create?
- What's the emotional core of this project?
- What 3 things must I remember as we move forward?"
